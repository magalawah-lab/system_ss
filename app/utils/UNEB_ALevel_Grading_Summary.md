# UNEB A-Level (UACE) Grading Guide

This document summarizes how individual paper grades (D1 to F9) combine to form the final overall principal subject grades (A to F) under the **Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB)**.

---

## 📌 Individual Paper Grading Scale
Individual exam papers are scored using the standard Ugandan 9-point grading scale:
*   **D1, D2:** Distinction (Highest)
*   **C3, C4, C5, C6:** Credit
*   **P7, P8:** Pass
*   **F9:** Fail

---

## 📊 Subjects with Two (2) Papers
*Applicable to most humanities and arts (e.g., Economics, History, Geography, Entrepreneurship).*

| Final Subject Grade | Required Paper Combinations (Order does not matter) |
| :---: | :--- |
| **A** | Both papers must be distinctions: `(1,1)`, `(1,2)`, or `(2,2)` |
| **B** | At worst a `C3` in one paper: `(1,3)`, `(2,3)`, or `(3,3)` |
| **C** | At worst a `C4` in one paper: `(1,4)`, `(2,4)`, `(3,4)`, or `(4,4)` |
| **D** | At worst a `C5` in one paper: `(1,5)`, `(2,5)`, `(3,5)`, `(4,5)`, or `(5,5)` |
| **E** | Lowest Principal Pass. At worst a `C6` in one paper: `(1,6)`, `(2,6)`, `(3,6)`, `(4,6)`, `(5,6)`, or `(6,6)` |
| **O** | Subsidiary Pass. One or both papers drop into passes: e.g., `(6,7)`, `(7,7)`, `(4,8)` |
| **F** | Fail. Severe failures in both papers: e.g., `(8,9)`, `(9,9)` |

---

## 📊 Subjects with Three (3) Papers
*Applicable to sciences and technical subjects (e.g., Physics, Chemistry, Biology).*

*   **Grade A:** Requires at least two distinctions and at worst a `C3` in the third paper (e.g., `1,1,3` or `1,2,3`). Three distinctions (`1,1,1` or `2,2,2`) automatically guarantee an **A**.
*   **Grades B to E (Principal Passes):** Balanced across all three papers. UNEB allows a single weak paper to be offset by two strong papers. For instance, a poor grade in a practical paper will pull your aggregate down but won't cause you to fail the entire subject if your theory papers are strong.
*   **Subsidiary Pass (O):** Achieved when the overall mathematical aggregation fails to hit the Principal 'E' threshold but shows basic minimum competence across the components.
